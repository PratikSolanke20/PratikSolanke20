## 👨‍💻 Executive Summary

I am a **Full App & Web Developer** and **Data Specialist** with a proven track record of designing, building, and deploying mission-critical digital products. My engineering expertise spans from **offline-first native Android applications** deployed to the Google Play Store, to **scalable web systems** (React, Next.js, Node.js, NestJS), and **browser-based Edge AI engines** (TensorFlow.js, MediaPipe, OpenCV).

Whether implementing zero-data-loss synchronization engines for healthcare platforms, architecting zero-latency hand-gesture vision pipelines, or building robust enterprise automation bots with Playwright and Python, I focus on **clean architecture, strict type-safety, unmatched speed, and intuitive UX**.
